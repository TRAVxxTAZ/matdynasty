Mat Dynasty: College Wrestling

Support

Need help? Email traviskj93@gmail.com. Include your device model and iOS version. In the app, Settings → Help & Diagnostics → Diagnostic Report copies a short report you can paste into your email.

Common questions

  • Restore purchases. Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases also work across devices signed in to that Apple ID, and with Family Sharing.
  • Where are my saves? Saves live only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so keep it installed to keep your dynasty.
  • Importing a world. Open a .cwc-world file from Files, Messages, or Mail and choose Mat Dynasty, or use Settings → Custom Worlds → Import a World. Importing is free and starts a new save in an empty slot. Your existing saves aren't touched.
  • Does it need internet? No. The whole game plays offline. The App Store is only needed for purchases and restores.
