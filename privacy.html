Privacy Policy

Mat Dynasty: College Wrestling · Effective October 27, 2026

Mat Dynasty does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

No data collection

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or SDKs. Nothing about you or how you play is sent anywhere.

Your saves stay on your device

Game data is stored locally on your iPhone. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions.

World files you share

If you export a world file and share it, you choose where it goes. The file contains only game data you created.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
